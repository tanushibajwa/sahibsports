About Sahib Sports

Our story, mission, and passion for sports

Our Story

Founded in 2015, Sahib Sports started as a small sports equipment store in Chandigarh with a simple mission: to provide athletes of all levels with access to high-quality sports gear at affordable prices.

What began as a dream shared by two sports enthusiasts has grown into one of the region's most trusted sports retailers. Over the years, we have served thousands of athletes, from school children taking their first swing to professional players competing at national levels.

Our name "Sahib" reflects our commitment to treating every customer with respect and providing service that is truly fit for champions.
